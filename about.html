Our Journey

Providing boutique clinical hygiene solutions for more than 15 years.

Shades of Emerald Eco Limited has been widely recognized for our commitment to environmental responsibility and surgical-grade precision. We have a history of excellence and dedication to our clients.

Our first clinical contract was signed in 2008, setting the foundation for our expertise in biohazard restoration.

We started as a small, specialized team focused on high-end residential cleaning in London. Our early commitment to eco-friendly products and meticulous attention to detail quickly set us apart in a market dominated by harsh chemical solutions.

As we evolved, we expanded into commercial and clinical sectors. In 2014, we launched our own manufacturing line of sustainable cleaning solutions to ensure maximum effectiveness without environmental compromise or safety risks.

Today, we continue to innovate under the leadership of our founding team. We are excited to announce a new era of growth, expanding our boutique services across the UK while maintaining our core values of clinical excellence and trust.
