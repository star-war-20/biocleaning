Innovation FAQs

Understanding our scientific approach.

Are your formulations REACH compliant?

Yes, all Shades of Emerald formulations strictly adhere to REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals) regulations, ensuring the highest level of safety for human health and the environment.

What is the environmental impact of your packaging?

We use 100% post-consumer recycled (PCR) plastics and infinitely recyclable aluminum for our packaging. Additionally, we offer bulk refill systems to minimize plastic waste across our service cycle.

Can we license your proprietary formulas?

Yes. We offer strategic licensing agreements for professional cleaning organizations and hospitality chains that wish to integrate our "Shades of Emerald" green chemistry into their own operations.
